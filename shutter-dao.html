Shutter DAO 0x36 is a true decentralized onchain organization, launched and canonized by community members

It provides grant funding to Shutter ecosystem projects and elects Keypers, the decentralized group that generates and safeguards the cryptographic keys enabling Shutter’s encrypted mempools, shielded voting, and API
